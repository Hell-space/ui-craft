Kiln Design System · Component specimen

Quiet tools for studios that work in fire.

Kiln schedules firings, tracks every shelf of ware through bisque and glaze, and tells a twelve-person ceramics studio whose pots are ready to collect. This sheet is the reference for its interface: every component, every state, in both themes.

12Dialog

Rendered open inside a stage so it can be studied. The title is the question; the body states the consequence in numbers; the destructive button repeats the verb.

Abort firing F-2290?

Kiln B is at 1,186 °C. Aborting cuts power and lets it cool naturally. The 38 pieces on board will be underfired and need a refire.

14Empty state

Says what will be here, why it's empty, and the one thing to do next. The drawing is in the product's own vocabulary — test tiles on a shelf — not a generic cloud.

15Chinese text 中文排版

Han characters use the platform's system UI face; Latin and numerals inside Chinese still come from Instrument Sans so "1,222 °C" looks the same in both languages. Line-height opens up to 1.75–1.87 and tracking to +2%.
