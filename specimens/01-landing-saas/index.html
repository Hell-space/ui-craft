    Talk to us
    Questions

    Things researchers ask us first.

    Something else? Email research@verbatim.app — a researcher on our team will answer, usually the same day.

    Will this replace our researchers?
    No, and it isn't designed to. Verbatim handles transcription, first-pass tagging, and filing — the parts that eat a researcher's week. Deciding what a pattern means, and what to do about it, stays with people. Every auto-drafted note is marked as a draft until someone reviews it.
    How accurate are the auto-drafted notes?
    Last quarter, researchers kept 92% of drafted notes without edits. Because every note is anchored to a quote and a timestamp, the other 8% are quick to catch: if the quote doesn't support the note, you'll see it immediately.
    We already use a research repository. Can we migrate?
    Yes. We import highlights, tags, and transcripts via CSV or API, and re-link them to recordings where the source files are available. Team and Enterprise plans include a guided migration.
    Do participants need to consent to recording?
    Yes — and Verbatim helps you track it. Each interview stores its consent record, and you can set retention rules that delete recordings automatically after a study closes while keeping anonymized notes.
    What counts as a “researcher” seat?
    Anyone who uploads interviews, edits notes, or manages templates. People who only search, read, play clips, or comment are free and unlimited on every plan.
